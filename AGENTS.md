# Project orientation

Before planning or changing training, evaluation, or research experiments, read
[RESEARCH_DIRECTION.md](RESEARCH_DIRECTION.md) and the relevant parts of
[README.md](README.md).

Use **Outcome-based RL** for the project's current final-answer-reward training
direction. Preserve the three agreed research questions: solving, judging, and
correction. Keep confirmed decisions, proposed experiments, and implemented
capabilities distinct; the research roadmap alone does not authorize running
every proposed experiment. Reference PDFs and papers are non-binding.

Follow the user's latest explicit instructions. When a research decision changes
or a planned capability is implemented, update the relevant documentation so
future sessions inherit the current direction.
